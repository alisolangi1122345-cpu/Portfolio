ABOUT

Where signal meets substance

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Khwaja Fareed University of Engineering & Information Technology (KFUEIT), based in Rahim Yar Khan, Pakistan. My work sits at the intersection of firmware and copper — I design embedded systems on Arduino and ESP32, then take them from breadboard to a fabricated PCB in KiCad and EasyEDA.

I simulate before I solder: Proteus catches the mistakes a multimeter would find later. Most of what I know comes from personal projects and late nights after lectures — a Bluetooth-controlled robotic car, a couple of regulated bench power supplies, and a few IoT automation experiments that didn't work on the first try. I share what I build online mostly to keep myself accountable and to look back on later.

I'm still learning, but I try to understand a circuit properly before calling it done — the right fuse, the right trace width, the right margin at 230V/50Hz — rather than just getting it to work once on the bench.
